# Sources and editable defaults

Research and endpoint checks were performed on 2026-09-20.

- [ЈУДГ Детска радост, Skopje: daily activities](https://gdr.mk/organizacija/dnevni-aktivnosti/) lists breakfast at 08:30, lunch at 11:30, rest from 12:30 and afternoon play/snacks. The app uses these as editable example times, not a national mandatory timetable. End times and the shorter default rest/snack pattern are prototype choices.
- [Municipality of Delchevo: kindergartens](https://www.delcevo.gov.mk/detski-gradinki) shows that local schedules vary. Each teacher should adapt the group’s schedule.
- [Ministry of Economy and Labour: 2026 non-working-day programme](https://www.economy.gov.mk/build/assets/programa-nerabotni-denovi-2026-MHaAbYyO.pdf) supplies the verified 27-event snapshot in `backend/config/holiday-snapshot.js`, with public and community-specific scopes. The programme's Republic Day substitute-date typo is corrected using the [separate official announcement](https://www.economy.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/soopstenie-za-neraboten-den753), which explicitly gives Sunday 2 August and Monday 3 August 2026. The fallback records this correction's source separately.
- The [Dormition announcement](https://www.economy.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/soopstenie-za-neraboten-den75) omits the year from its paragraph. Its year is inferred from the same article's 24/08/2026 publication badge on the official index, with a matching weekday and a maximum 45-day publication-to-holiday interval. This inference is recorded as `yearBasis: publication_date`; it never uses the computer's current year.
- The [official announcements index](https://www.economy.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija) is scraped on the server. Supported holiday announcements are parsed from their article bodies, with the year, calendar date, weekday, scope and source validated. [Orthodox Easter Monday](https://www.economy.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/soopstenie-za-neraboten-den52), [Catholic Easter Monday](https://www.economy.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/soopstenie-za-neraboten-den14) and [Orthodox Good Friday](https://www.economy.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/soopstenie-za-neraboten-den69) are known discovery entry points. Dates are read from the announcements, not calculated by the model.
- Halloween (31 October), Earth Day (22 April), and World Environment Day (5 June) are explicitly curated, optional classroom occasions, not scraped Macedonian public holidays. Teachers confirm actual kindergarten closures.
- [pulse.eco city directory](https://pulse.eco/) and [REST API](https://pulse.eco/restapi) provide catalog/current/history endpoints. Catalogs returned HTTP 200 for Skopje, Bitola, Kumanovo, Prilep, Tetovo, Veles, Shtip, Ohrid, Gostivar, Strumica, Kichevo, Kochani, Struga, Radovish, Negotino, Resen, Krushevo, Bogdanci, Star Dojran and Novo Selo. Skopje has five neighbourhood centres, yielding 24 selectable locations. Catalog existence does not guarantee two valid nearby sensors today.
- [Open-Meteo weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) and [air-quality API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api) document forecasts, WMO weather codes and CAMS coverage. Visible attribution remains in the weather/calendar and air-source disclosure.
- [EPA AQI technical assistance, May 2024](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P101AP0Q.TXT), [school outdoor activity guidance](https://document.airnow.gov/air-quality-and-outdoor-guidance-for-schools.pdf), and [sensor quality assurance](https://www.epa.gov/air-sensor-toolbox/quality-assurance-air-sensors) inform the deterministic screening policy described in DECISION_BASIS.md.
- [Official OpenAI structured-output guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs) documents Responses `text.format` with strict JSON Schema. Server validation remains necessary.
- [Preact’s no-build setup](https://preactjs.com/guide/v10/getting-started/) documents browser ES modules and HTM. This app vendors them locally rather than loading a runtime CDN.
- [Node SQLite documentation](https://nodejs.org/api/sqlite.html) describes the built-in database used for accounts and plans.

The activity library, translations, scheduler, visual design and SVG children were created for this prototype. They are examples, not an official Macedonian curriculum or evidence from a real teacher.
